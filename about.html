01 — WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN
AN EVENT.

REMOVERSE is an inter-college fest presented by BCA, School of Information Technology, Guru Nanak College (Autonomous), Chennai.

Built around the energy of student talent, REMOVERSE brings participants together to explore ideas, challenge themselves, and share the excitement of friendly competition. From technical thinking to non-technical creativity, the fest is a platform to participate, connect, and make your moment count.

Whether you come to compete, collaborate, or cheer on your friends, you are part of the experience. Discover the events, review each event’s rules, and complete your registration through the official event pages.
